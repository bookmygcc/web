How engagements are billed

The GCC AI Governance Diagnostic is billed in two instalments: 50% on signing the engagement agreement, and the remaining 50% on delivery of the board-ready pack, around working day 15.

One clear cutoff: the kick-off call

We keep this simple, with a single cutoff rather than a sliding scale.

Cancelling before kick-off

If you cancel any time before the kick-off call, we refund your signing instalment in full, less any payment-processing fees already incurred.

Cancelling after kick-off

Once the kick-off call has happened, the signing instalment is earned and non-refundable, in full, regardless of how much of the 15 working days remain. There is no pro-rating by day or by work completed. The second instalment is only ever invoiced for the board-ready pack we actually deliver; we do not invoice you for work not delivered.

If we cannot deliver

If we are unable to complete the diagnostic for reasons on our side, we refund the instalment for the undelivered portion of the work in full, whichever side of the kick-off call that falls on.

How refunds are paid

Refunds are paid to the original paying account, normally within 10 business days of agreement on the amount.
